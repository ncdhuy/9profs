# Master's Thesis Purpose and Depth

## `thesis-as-integrated-research-work`
A master's thesis should function as an integrated research work rather than a collection of loosely related chapters. Its parts should contribute to a recognizable research purpose.

## `master-level-depth`
A master's thesis should normally demonstrate more than summarizing information: sustained engagement with a defined problem, informed use of scholarship, defensible research choices, organized evidence, interpretation, awareness of limitations, and justified conclusions.

## `independent-reasoning`
A thesis should contain visible intellectual work by the researcher through problem formulation, synthesis, justification of approach, interpretation, comparison, treatment of uncertainty, or development of implications.

## `artifact-coherence`
The thesis should read as one research artifact. Weak coherence includes literature unrelated to the investigation, methods introducing concepts absent from framing, results disconnected from objectives, or conclusions functioning as a new essay.
