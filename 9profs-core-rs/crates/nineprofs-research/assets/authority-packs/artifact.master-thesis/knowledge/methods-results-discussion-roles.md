# Methods, Results, and Discussion as Thesis Components

## `methods-as-research-account`
Methods should provide a sufficiently developed account of how the research was performed and why consequential choices were made.

## `methods-detail-proportionality`
Detail should be proportional to importance for understanding/evaluation; maximal length is not the goal.

## `results-as-organized-answer`
Results should be organized around the research purpose, questions, objectives, themes, or outcomes.

## `results-description-vs-analysis`
Where the question requires explanation, comparison, evaluation, or interpretation, description alone may be insufficient.

## `discussion-adds-intellectual-value`
Discussion should add intellectual value beyond repeating results or listing prior studies.

## `discussion-return-to-literature`
Relevant literature developed earlier should contribute to interpretation later.

## `discussion-honors-results`
The thesis's own results should remain the principal object of interpretation.
