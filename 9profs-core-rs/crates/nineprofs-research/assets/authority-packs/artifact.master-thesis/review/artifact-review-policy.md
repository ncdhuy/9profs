# Master's Thesis Artifact Review Policy

- `artifact-not-regulation`: generic thesis expectations do not determine formal institutional compliance.
- `artifact-semantic-roles`: evaluate semantic function before chapter label.
- `artifact-depth-not-length`: do not infer quality from page count.
- `artifact-no-universal-template`: do not require IMRaD, five chapters, or another fixed template unless another authority requires it.
- `artifact-authority-boundary`: do not use this pack for specialized technical validity.
- `artifact-overlap-with-core`: use `research.core` for universal logic; use this pack for depth, completeness, integration, or scholarly role expected specifically of a master's thesis.
