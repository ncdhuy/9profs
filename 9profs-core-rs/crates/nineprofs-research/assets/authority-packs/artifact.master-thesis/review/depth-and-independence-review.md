# Review Guidance — Master's-Level Depth and Independent Reasoning

## Goal
Evaluate whether the artifact demonstrates sufficient analytical and scholarly engagement rather than simple compilation or description.

## Look especially for
- `thesis-depth/descriptive-only`
- `thesis-depth/source-dependent`
- `thesis-depth/unjustified-choice`
- `thesis-depth/thin-discussion`
- `thesis-depth/unsupported-contribution`

Do not flag legitimately descriptive research merely for being descriptive.
