# Review Guidance — Thesis-Level Synthesis

## Goal
Evaluate the thesis as a complete artifact rather than isolated sections.

Review whether the trajectory problem → objectives → investigation → findings → interpretation → conclusion is traceable; whether literature supports framing and interpretation; whether the artifact demonstrates master's-level analysis; and whether the conclusion completes the research argument.

## Look especially for
- `thesis-synthesis/chapter-isolation`
- `thesis-synthesis/intellectual-gap`
- `thesis-synthesis/literature-result-gap`
- `thesis-synthesis/result-discussion-gap`
- `thesis-synthesis/contribution-gap`
