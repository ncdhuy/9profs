# Review Guidance — Literature and Scholarly Foundation

## Goal
Evaluate whether literature and conceptual material establish a useful scholarly foundation.

## Look especially for
- `thesis-literature/catalogue`
- `thesis-literature/relevance`
- `thesis-literature/gap-jump`
- `thesis-literature/conceptual-disconnect`
- `thesis-literature/discussion-disconnect`

Do not determine literature completeness or novelty solely from the manuscript; use evidence retrieval when necessary.
