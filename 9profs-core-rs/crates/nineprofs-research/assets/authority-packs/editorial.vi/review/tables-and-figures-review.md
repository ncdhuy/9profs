# Review Guidance — Tables and Figures

## Goal
Evaluate whether tables and figures communicate research information clearly and integrate appropriately with the manuscript.

## Look especially for
- `vi-table/unclear-caption`
- `vi-table/undefined-element`
- `vi-table/text-duplication`
- `vi-table/text-conflict`
- `vi-table/unintegrated`
- `vi-table/semantic-mismatch`
