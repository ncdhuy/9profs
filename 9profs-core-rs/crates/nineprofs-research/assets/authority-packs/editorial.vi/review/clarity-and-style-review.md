# Review Guidance — Clarity and Academic Style

## Goal
Evaluate whether Vietnamese academic prose communicates scientific meaning clearly, precisely, and efficiently.

## Look especially for
- `vi-style/unclear-sentence`
- `vi-style/ambiguous-reference`
- `vi-style/redundancy`
- `vi-style/bureaucratic-expression`
- `vi-style/unsupported-emphasis`
- `vi-style/paragraph-coherence`
