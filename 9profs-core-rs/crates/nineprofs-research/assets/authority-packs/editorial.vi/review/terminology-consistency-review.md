# Review Guidance — Terminology and Consistency

## Goal
Evaluate whether technical language remains stable and unambiguous.

## Look especially for
- `vi-term/inconsistent-term`
- `vi-term/translation-drift`
- `vi-term/undefined-abbreviation`
- `vi-term/abbreviation-conflict`
- `vi-term/entity-name-drift`

Do not normalize terms automatically when they may represent scientifically different concepts.
