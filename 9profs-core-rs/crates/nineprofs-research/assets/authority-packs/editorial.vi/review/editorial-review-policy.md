# Vietnamese Editorial Review Policy

- `editorial-preserve-science`: preserve scientific meaning; if intent is unclear, ask for clarification rather than guess.
- `editorial-vs-scientific`: distinguish poor wording from poor scientific reasoning.
- `editorial-no-content-invention`: do not add missing results, rationale, evidence, references, clinical explanations, or new interpretation.
- `editorial-materiality`: prioritize issues materially affecting readability, ambiguity, tone, precision, terminology, interpretation, or consistency.
- `editorial-rewrite-safety`: propose edits only when original meaning is clear and scientific scope/uncertainty are preserved.
- `editorial-deterministic-first`: prefer deterministic correction for safe mechanical issues.
