# Citation and Reference Language

## `citation-integration`
Citations should integrate grammatically into Vietnamese prose and make the supported proposition reasonably clear.

## `citation-scope`
Citation placement should clarify which statements are supported when paragraphs contain multiple claims.

## `citation-as-substitute-for-reasoning`
A citation does not replace explanation or comparison.

## `citation-style-boundary`
`editorial.vi` does not determine Vancouver, APA, IEEE, Harvard, or another citation style.

## `foreign-language-term`
Foreign terms may be retained when necessary for precision or disciplinary convention, but unnecessary switching can reduce readability.
