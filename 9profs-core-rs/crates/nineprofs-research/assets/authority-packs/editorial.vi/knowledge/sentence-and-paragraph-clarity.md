# Sentence and Paragraph Clarity

## `one-discernible-main-idea`
A sentence may contain multiple clauses, but its main proposition should remain identifiable.

## `long-sentence-is-not-automatically-bad`
Judge comprehension and logical structure rather than arbitrary length thresholds.

## `subject-predicate-clarity`
Subject-predicate relationships should be clear. Constructions such as "Qua kết quả nghiên cứu cho thấy..." often need revision.

## `reference-clarity`
Words such as này, đó, điều này, kết quả này should have identifiable referents.

## `logical-connectors`
Connectors such as tuy nhiên, do đó, vì vậy, ngoài ra, đồng thời, mặc dù should reflect actual logical relations.

## `paragraph-unity`
Paragraphs should develop a recognizable idea/function and avoid unrelated aggregation.

## `paragraph-progression`
Paragraphs should show coherent development rather than sentence accumulation.

## `avoid-repetition`
Distinguish necessary technical repetition from unnecessary semantic duplication.

## `avoid-meaning-loss-in-rewrite`
Editorial improvement must preserve population, effect direction, uncertainty, temporal relationship, causal strength, comparison, numbers, and scope.
