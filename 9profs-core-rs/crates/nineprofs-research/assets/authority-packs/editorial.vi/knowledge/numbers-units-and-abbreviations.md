# Numbers, Units, and Abbreviations

## `numeric-meaning`
Present numbers consistently and unambiguously; consider units, decimal representation, percentages, ranges, dates, and sample counts.

## `number-unit-link`
Measurements should clearly associate values with required units.

## `unit-consistency`
Comparable quantities should use compatible units or explicit conversion/explanation.

## `decimal-consistency`
Decimal separators and representation should remain consistent with selected convention/regulation; do not rewrite values in ways that risk changing meaning.

## `percentage-count-consistency`
Percentages and counts should correspond when both are presented.

## `range-clarity`
Ranges should clearly show endpoints and units where relevant.

## `abbreviation-density`
Excessive abbreviations can reduce readability; use them when they improve clarity.

## `deterministic-candidates`
Good sensor candidates include undefined abbreviations, duplicate definitions, inconsistent expansion, missing/ inconsistent units, repeated numeric mismatches, percentage/count mismatch, multiple spaces, punctuation spacing, and repeated punctuation.
