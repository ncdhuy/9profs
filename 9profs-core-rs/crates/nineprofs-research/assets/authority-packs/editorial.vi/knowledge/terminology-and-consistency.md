# Terminology and Consistency

## `terminology-stability`
Technical terminology should remain sufficiently stable to avoid conceptual ambiguity.

## `preferred-term`
Where several expressions refer to the same concept, establish a preferred term and use it consistently unless variation is harmless.

## `translation-consistency`
Translations of technical terms should remain consistent unless distinctions are intentional.

## `first-use-clarity`
Specialized terms, abbreviations, or translated concepts should be introduced clearly enough for intended readers.

## `abbreviation-consistency`
Avoid abbreviations used before definition, repeated definitions, conflicting abbreviations, or unpredictable switching.

## `technical-term-vs-common-language`
Do not replace technical terms with approximate everyday wording when precision is lost.

## `entity-consistency`
Names of organizations, institutions, diseases, medicines, instruments, scales, datasets, and study groups should remain consistent unless multiple official forms are relevant.
