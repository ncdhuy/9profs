# Headings, Tables, and Figures

## `heading-semantic-hierarchy`
Headings should reveal logical hierarchy and should not be used merely for visual emphasis.

## `heading-parallelism`
Headings at the same level should use reasonably parallel naming conventions.

## `table-self-sufficiency`
Tables should normally be understandable with clear title, variables, units, group labels, abbreviations/notes, and denominators where relevant.

## `table-text-relationship`
Narrative should emphasize important patterns rather than mechanically reproduce the table.

## `figure-self-sufficiency`
Figures should clearly identify variables, groups, axes, units, and legends.

## `table-figure-reference`
Important tables/figures should be integrated into the manuscript narrative.

## `caption-consistency`
Captions should use consistent terminology and numbering. Deterministic sensors can detect duplicate/missing numbers, broken references, and caption-format inconsistencies.
