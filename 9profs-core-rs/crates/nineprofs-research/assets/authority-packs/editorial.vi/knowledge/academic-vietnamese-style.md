# Vietnamese Academic Style

## `academic-register`
Academic Vietnamese should prioritize clarity, precision, neutrality, logical continuity, terminological stability, and economy of expression. Complexity is not a proxy for scholarship.

## `precision-over-decoration`
Prefer precise meaning over rhetorical decoration. Vague, emotional, promotional, or excessively emphatic expressions should be avoided unless defined by evidence/context.

## `objective-tone`
Maintain an objective, professional tone. Unsupported subjective language should not replace evidence.

## `avoid-unnecessary-personalization`
Expressions such as "theo chúng tôi" can be useful for distinguishing interpretation but should not replace explicit reasoning.

## `directness`
Remove words that do not add semantic value and prefer direct academic phrasing.

## `avoid-bureaucratic-prose`
Avoid unnecessary administrative constructions such as "nhằm mục đích để", "trên cơ sở căn cứ vào", or "tiến hành thực hiện" when simpler wording preserves meaning.

## `academic-caution`
Use qualified language when appropriate to represent evidential strength; qualification should not be added mechanically.
