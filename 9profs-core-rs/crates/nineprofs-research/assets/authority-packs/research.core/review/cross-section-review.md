# Review Guidance — Cross-Section Consistency

## Goal
Evaluate whether the manuscript remains semantically and factually coherent across sections.

Compare, when present:
Title ↔ Abstract ↔ Objectives ↔ Methods ↔ Results ↔ Discussion ↔ Conclusion

## Look especially for
- `cross-section/objective-drift`
- `cross-section/entity-drift`
- `cross-section/result-drift`
- `cross-section/numeric-conflict`
- `cross-section/conclusion-drift`

A contradiction indicates at least one representation requires correction; do not decide which without adequate evidence.
