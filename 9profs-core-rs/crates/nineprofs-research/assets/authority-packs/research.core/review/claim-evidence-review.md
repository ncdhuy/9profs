# Review Guidance — Claims and Evidence

## Goal
Evaluate whether important scientific claims are supported at an appropriate level and whether evidence is distinguished from inference.

## Look especially for
- `claim-review/unsupported`: important assertions have no visible evidential basis.
- `claim-review/overstated`: wording expresses greater certainty, scope, causality, or generality than support permits.
- `claim-review/observation-inference`: inference/explanation is presented as directly observed.
- `claim-review/evidence-mismatch`: evidence does not logically support the specific claim.
- `claim-review/novelty`: strong novelty assertions lack adequate support.

When external literature must be checked, use evidence retrieval rather than guessing from citation metadata.
