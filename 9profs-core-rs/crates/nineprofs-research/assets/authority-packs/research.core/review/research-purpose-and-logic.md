# Review Guidance — Research Purpose and Logic

## Goal
Evaluate whether the manuscript forms a coherent research argument from problem to conclusion.

## Review focus
Inspect what is being investigated, why, what objectives/questions guide the work, whether the work addresses them, whether results answer them, and whether conclusions follow.

## Look especially for
- `purpose-logic/problem-objective`: objectives poorly connected to the stated problem.
- `purpose-logic/objective-work`: research work cannot meaningfully address an objective.
- `purpose-logic/objective-results`: objectives have no corresponding results.
- `purpose-logic/results-conclusion`: conclusions unsupported by reported results or broader than the evidence base.

Do not determine specialized methodological appropriateness unless another authority pack supplies the necessary knowledge.
