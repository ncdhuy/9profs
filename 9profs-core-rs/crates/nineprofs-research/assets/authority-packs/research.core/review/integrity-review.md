# Review Guidance — Integrity and Transparency

## Goal
Identify issues that obstruct transparent scientific reasoning without unsupported misconduct allegations.

## Look especially for
- `integrity/missing-critical-information`
- `integrity/unexplained-contradiction`
- `integrity/unsupported-certainty`
- `integrity/source-dependence`
- `integrity/implausibility`

When uncertain whether there is an error or insufficient reporting, prefer the narrower diagnosis: the manuscript does not provide enough information to determine...
