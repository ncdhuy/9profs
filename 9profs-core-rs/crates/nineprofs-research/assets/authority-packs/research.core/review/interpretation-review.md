# Review Guidance — Interpretation and Conclusions

## Goal
Evaluate whether interpretation is disciplined and conclusions remain within evidential boundaries.

## Look especially for
- `interpretation/speculation-as-fact`
- `interpretation/selective-certainty`
- `interpretation/literature-comparison`
- `interpretation/limitation-disconnect`
- `interpretation/overgeneralization`
- `interpretation/recommendation-jump`

Recommend qualification, clarification of inference, acknowledgment of uncertainty, or narrower scope when appropriate. Do not manufacture new evidence.
