# Cross-Section Consistency

## `semantic-identity`

Important concepts should retain stable meaning throughout the manuscript, including objectives, population, exposures/interventions, outcomes, variables, periods, geographic scope, datasets, groups, concepts, and definitions.

## `objective-traceability`

Important objectives should be traceable through objective → method/analysis → result → interpretation → conclusion. Unexplained breaks can indicate substantive problems.

## `numerical-consistency`

Values describing the same fact should not contradict one another across sections without explanation. Deterministic sensors may assist extraction and comparison.

## `scope-consistency`

Scope introduced in one section should not silently expand or contract elsewhere.

## `temporal-consistency`

Research timeframes should remain coherent; conflicting periods or conclusions about periods not analyzed should be flagged.

## `terminology-consistency`

Terminology should be sufficiently consistent to preserve conceptual clarity. Pure language/style consistency belongs primarily to editorial packs.
