# Research Integrity and Transparency

## `traceable-reasoning`

A manuscript should expose enough reasoning for a qualified reader to understand how consequential conclusions were reached.

## `do-not-invent`

Missing information must remain missing unless recovered from an authoritative source. Never fabricate data, citations, methods, sample characteristics, analyses, results, ethical approvals, author intentions, explanations, or source content.

## `ambiguity-vs-error`

Ambiguous reporting and substantive error are different diagnoses. Preserve the distinction.

## `contradiction`

Internal contradiction signals a problem but does not reveal which statement is correct. Identify the inconsistency instead of arbitrarily selecting one version.

## `source-discipline`

Distinguish citation exists, source is retrievable, source contains the claimed information, and source supports the interpretation. These are separate questions.

## `researcher-intent`

Do not infer undocumented researcher intent when multiple interpretations are possible.

## `integrity-signals-not-verdicts`

Implausible values, missing provenance, duplication, or unsupported claims may warrant investigation but should not automatically be labeled fabrication, plagiarism, falsification, or misconduct.
