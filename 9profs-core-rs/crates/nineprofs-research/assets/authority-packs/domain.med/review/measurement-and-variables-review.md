# Review Guidance — Variables and Measurement

## Goal
Evaluate whether important constructs, variables, exposures, interventions, and outcomes are defined/measured clearly enough to support interpretation.

## Look especially for
- `med-measurement/construct-mismatch`
- `med-measurement/definition-ambiguity`
- `med-measurement/timing`
- `med-measurement/instrument-support`
- `med-measurement/source-limitations`
- `med-measurement/missing-data`
