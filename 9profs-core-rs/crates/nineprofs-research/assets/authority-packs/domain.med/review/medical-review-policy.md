# Medicine & Health Review Policy

- `med-domain-boundary`: use `domain.med` for health/biomedical reasoning; `research.core` for universal logic.
- `med-no-universal-design-hierarchy`: no study design is universally superior.
- `med-no-clinical-advice`: manuscript review must not become patient-specific clinical advice.
- `med-diagnose-carefully`: prefer precise diagnoses over sweeping invalidity claims.
- `med-reporting-vs-validity`: distinguish not reported, unclear, appears inappropriate, and demonstrably invalid.
- `med-specialized-authority`: defer detailed trial, diagnostic, biostatistical, guideline, meta-analysis, or clinical judgments to specialized authority.
- `med-materiality`: prioritize issues affecting interpretation, validity, clinical meaning, generalizability, confidence, reproducibility, or ethics.
