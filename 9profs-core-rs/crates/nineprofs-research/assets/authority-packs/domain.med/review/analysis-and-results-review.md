# Review Guidance — Analysis and Results

## Goal
Evaluate whether analysis addresses the medical research question and whether results are interpreted at an appropriate evidential level.

## Look especially for
- `med-analysis/objective-analysis-gap`
- `med-analysis/descriptive-inferential-jump`
- `med-analysis/significance-only`
- `med-analysis/estimate-certainty`
- `med-analysis/model-causal-jump`
- `med-analysis/exploratory-certainty`
- `med-analysis/result-inconsistency`
