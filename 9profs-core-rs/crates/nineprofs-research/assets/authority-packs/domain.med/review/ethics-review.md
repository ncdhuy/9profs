# Review Guidance — Ethics and Human Subjects

## Goal
Identify whether ethical and human-subject considerations are reported sufficiently without unsupported accusations.

## Look especially for
- `med-ethics/approval-unclear`
- `med-ethics/consent-unclear`
- `med-ethics/privacy`
- `med-ethics/vulnerability`

Do not transform "ethical approval is not reported" into "the study was conducted without ethical approval" unless evidence establishes that conclusion.
