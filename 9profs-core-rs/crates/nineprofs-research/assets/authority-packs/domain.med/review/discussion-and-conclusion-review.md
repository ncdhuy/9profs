# Review Guidance — Medical Interpretation and Conclusion

## Goal
Evaluate whether medical/health findings are interpreted with appropriate scientific and clinical discipline.

## Look especially for
- `med-interpretation/association-causation`
- `med-interpretation/clinical-overstatement`
- `med-interpretation/mechanism-speculation`
- `med-interpretation/generalization`
- `med-interpretation/individual-population`
- `med-interpretation/recommendation-jump`
- `med-interpretation/limitation-disconnect`
