# Review Guidance — Design and Population

## Goal
Evaluate whether study design and population are logically appropriate for the health research question and whether the population supporting conclusions is sufficiently clear.

## Look especially for
- `med-design/question-design-mismatch`
- `med-population/unclear-target`
- `med-population/sample-target-gap`
- `med-population/selection-process`
- `med-population/sample-size-rationale`
- `med-population/participant-flow`

Do not fabricate sample-size calculations.
