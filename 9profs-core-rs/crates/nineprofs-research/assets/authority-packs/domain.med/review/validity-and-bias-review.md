# Review Guidance — Validity, Bias, and Confounding

## Goal
Evaluate whether important threats to validity are identified and incorporated into interpretation.

Consider selection, measurement, confounding, missing data, implementation, analysis, reporting, and generalizability relative to design.

## Look especially for
- `med-validity/selection-bias`
- `med-validity/information-bias`
- `med-validity/confounding`
- `med-validity/adjustment-overconfidence`
- `med-validity/internal-validity`
- `med-validity/generalization`
- `med-validity/limitation-disconnect`
