# Biomedical Research Logic

## `med-research-question`
Medical and health research may concern frequency/distribution, characteristics, associations, determinants, diagnosis, prognosis, intervention effects, safety, healthcare delivery, medicine use, patient experience, health behavior, quality of care, health systems, or mechanisms. Different questions require different evidence.

## `design-follows-question`
The study design should be capable of generating evidence relevant to the research question. Detailed design-specific requirements belong to `study-design.*` packs.

## `clinical-vs-research-question`
A clinical concern establishes importance but does not by itself define the research question.

## `association-causation`
Association does not automatically establish causation or show that changing an exposure will prevent an outcome.

## `clinical-description-vs-explanation`
Describing patient or service characteristics is scientifically different from explaining why patterns occur.
