# Ethics and Human Subjects

## `ethics-is-contextual`
Ethical requirements depend on jurisdiction, institution, design, and regulation; this pack supplies semantic principles, not legal compliance determinations.

## `ethical-oversight`
Where review/approval is expected, the manuscript should make status sufficiently clear. Absence of reporting does not establish absence of approval.

## `informed-consent`
Where relevant, reporting should make clear how participant autonomy was addressed; legitimate waivers/exceptions may exist.

## `privacy-confidentiality`
Avoid unnecessary disclosure of identifiable health information and describe material protection measures where relevant.

## `risk-benefit`
Interventional/invasive research may require risk-benefit consideration; detailed assessment belongs to specialized authorities.

## `vulnerable-populations`
Potentially vulnerable populations may require additional protections.

## `ethics-reporting-vs-ethics-validity`
Absence of ethics reporting and unethical conduct are different findings.
