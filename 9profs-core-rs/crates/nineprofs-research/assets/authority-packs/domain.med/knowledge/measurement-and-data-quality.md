# Measurement and Data Quality

## `measurement-validity`
Measurements should meaningfully represent the construct/condition assessed. Concerns may include unclear definitions, inappropriate proxies, instruments outside intended context, inconsistent procedures, or unsupported thresholds.

## `measurement-reliability`
Measurement variability may arise from observers, instruments, laboratories, timing, recall, administration, or biology.

## `measurement-timing`
Timing can be scientifically important; technically correct measurements may mislead if timing is inconsistent with interpretation.

## `classification`
Categorization may lose information or introduce arbitrary boundaries; important categories should be clear and justified.

## `data-source`
Data provenance and source limitations should be sufficiently clear.

## `missing-data`
Consider amount, affected variables, likely mechanism, systematic differences, and handling of missingness.

## `data-cleaning-transformation`
Consequential exclusions, recoding, imputation, derived variables, extreme-value handling, aggregation, or normalization should be transparent.
